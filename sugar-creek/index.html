The only Arborium cabin with its own creek and 16 private acres — book it on its own, or add it to a whole-property reunion. It sits on its own gated 16.87 acres, one parcel over from Sunset, Stargazing, and Nightfall. A 2022 custom-built log home — 1,600 square feet, three bedrooms, two baths — and the reason you'll come back: it overlooks Sugar Creek, with wraparound porches facing the water.

Behind you, mature white oaks. Past the fence line, the Ouachita National Forest and the Caney Creek Wilderness. Soak in the hot tub while the creek runs below. Wolf Pen Gap is five miles up the road. Sugar Creek Cabin opens Fall 2027 — from $349/night. Join the waitlist and you'll hear first; full details and booking to come.
