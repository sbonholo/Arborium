Sugar Creek Cabin

Perched.
Private.
On the water.

Our fourth cabin sits on its own gated 16.87 acres, one parcel over from Sunset, Stargazing, and Nightfall. A 2022 custom-built log home — 1,600 square feet, three bedrooms, two baths — and the reason you'll come back: it's perched right over Sugar Creek, with wraparound porches hanging out over the water.

Behind you, mature white oaks. Past the fence line, the Ouachita National Forest and the Caney Creek Wilderness. Soak in the hot tub while the creek runs below. Wolf Pen Gap is five miles up the road. Sugar Creek Cabin opens soon — join the waitlist and you'll hear first.
