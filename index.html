The Escape

Secluded.
Private.
Unforgettable.

Ideal for couples, families, and friends. Nestled deep in the Ouachita National Forest near Wolf Pen Gap, this handcrafted timber-frame cabin is an ATV and SxS paradise — ride from the doorstep straight to the trails.

Hike, explore creeks, fish bass on the private pond, and watch the sun melt into the mountains at the Vista. Cozy up by the stone fireplace or gather around the fire pit under the stars. No cell service — just Starlink Wi-Fi, total privacy, and the sound of the forest.

Adventure Awaits

Built for those
who love the outdoors.

🏍️

ATV & SxS Riding

Ride directly from the cabin to the Wolf Pen Gap ATV trail system — one of Arkansas's premier off-road destinations. The perfect basecamp for a full weekend of riding.

🎣

Bass Fishing

Cast a line in the on-property bass pond or explore nearby Shady Lake and the creeks winding through the national forest. No drive needed — fish from your own backyard.

🥾

Hiking & Biking

Explore hundreds of miles of trails through the Ouachita National Forest. Visit Bard Springs, a hidden waterfall oasis, or summit a ridge for panoramic forest views.

🌅

Sunset at the Vista

Golden hour at the Vista is something guests return for again and again. Watch the sun set over an ocean of Ouachita treetops — a moment you won't find anywhere else.

💧

Creek Exploring

Wade through crystal-clear Ouachita creeks, discover natural swimming holes, and explore the hidden hollows of the national forest. Shady Lake is just minutes away.

🐻

Wildlife Watching

Deer graze at dusk, turkeys wander the property at dawn, and black bears have been spotted right on the grounds. This is their forest — you're just visiting.
